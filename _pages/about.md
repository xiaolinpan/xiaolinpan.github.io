I am a computational chemist working at the interface of **machine learning, quantum chemistry and drug discovery**. I am currently a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Chemistry at [New York University](https://www.nyu.edu/), working with Prof. Yingkai Zhang. In 2027, I will start an independent project at the **University of Vienna** as Principal Investigator of an FWF ESPRIT Fellowship, hosted by Prof. Johannes Kirchmair.

My research aims to make molecular modeling more reliable for real drug-discovery decisions: getting protonation states, tautomers and p*K*<sub>a</sub> right, and modeling how small molecules and peptides recognize their protein targets. I also care about turning methods into tools people use. I am the lead developer of [MolGpKa](https://github.com/Xundrug/MolGpKa), [MolTaut](https://github.com/Xundrug/MolTaut), [sPhysNet-Taut](https://github.com/xiaolinpan/sPhysNet-Taut) and [AA-Score](https://github.com/Xundrug/AA-Score-Tool), and the [Xundrug](https://xundrug.cn) platform hosting our web servers has received more than 1.1 million visits.

Before NYU, I was a Senior Scientist in computational drug discovery at WuXi AppTec. I received my Ph.D. in Physical Chemistry from East China Normal University with Profs. John Z. H. Zhang and Changge Ji, and my B.Eng. in Pharmaceutical Engineering from the University of Shanghai for Science and Technology.

#### Research Interests

- Physics-informed machine learning for molecular properties (protonation states, tautomers, p*K*<sub>a</sub>, metabolism)
- Protein–ligand and peptide–protein interaction modeling
- Structure-based lead optimization and prospective validation in blind challenges
- Open cheminformatics software and web servers
